02 / The bigger picture

What is
Hack Club?

Hack Club is a global nonprofit network of high-school makers and student-led coding clubs. Nearly 1,400 high schools and about 100,000 teenagers take part each year. Fox Valley Hackers is the Aurora chapter.

05 / Questions

You belong here.

Still unsure? That is a perfectly good place to start.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. We learn by making. Bring curiosity, not credentials.

What happens at a hackathon?

You pick an idea, meet people, build for a while, and share what you made. Finished is optional.

Can I lead something?

Yes. Start a club, organize a meetup, or lead a project.
